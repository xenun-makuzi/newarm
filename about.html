About Liberty Arms

Company facts are entered by the business and published only after approval.

Company History

Founded in 2021 in Houston, Texas, Liberty Arms began as a small private collection of classic American firearms. What started as a passion for precision engineering grew into a trusted name among collectors and enthusiasts. Over the years, we have expanded our network, sourcing from private collections and directly from manufacturers to bring our customers the finest selection of American-made firearms. Today, we serve customers across the country with the same dedication to quality that defined our first sale.

Mission and Values

Our mission is to provide responsible citizens with access to high-quality firearms while upholding the highest standards of safety and compliance. We believe in the Second Amendment without apology, and we believe in responsible ownership. Every firearm we sell is inspected, tested, and described honestly. We value integrity, transparency, and the trust of our customers. We do not compromise on quality, and we do not cut corners.

Business Approach

We operate as a private online retailer, sourcing firearms from trusted networks and offering them directly to buyers. Our approach is simple: we inspect every item personally, describe it accurately, and ship it discreetly. We do not maintain a physical showroom — our business is built on reputation and word of mouth. We prioritize privacy for our customers and efficiency in our operations. Every transaction is handled with care and professionalism.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Rigorous inspection — Every firearm passes a multi-point check before listing
  • Honest descriptions — We grade condition accurately, no hype
  • Discreet shipping — Plain packaging, no markings
  • Customer privacy — We never share your information
  • Texas roots — Proudly based in Houston, serving the nation
