Company overview

Liberty Arms is a Houston, Texas based online retailer specializing in American-made firearms and accessories. We are a federally licensed dealer (FFL) and our credentials are verified and published on this site. Every item we list has been personally inspected and function-tested. We handle orders discreetly. Our business details are added by the company and appear here once they have been approved.

Privacy and discretion

Buyer anonymity where the law allows. We do not sell, rent, or share your information for marketing. Account and order data are used only to complete your order and meet legal requirements. You can read the full details in our privacy policy.

Responsible ownership starts with knowing and following the laws that apply to you, storing items securely, and getting qualified training. Read our safety and responsibility guide.
